# Contributors

[English](CONTRIBUTORS.md) | [简体中文](CONTRIBUTORS.zh-CN.md)

NeuronMesh credits code, tests, benchmarks, protocol review, documentation, translations, compatibility reports and reproduced experiments. Contribution volume alone does not determine credit or decision authority.

## Maintainer

- [@yanxee](https://github.com/yanxee) — project maintainer and initial research implementation

## Research and community contributors

The first external contributors will be listed here after their work is merged or their reproducible artifact is accepted, unless they prefer to remain anonymous.

Ways to be recognized:

- a merged pull request;
- a published benchmark or negative result with a reproduction artifact;
- a reviewed protocol or threat model;
- a verified compatibility or deployment report;
- a documentation, translation or accessibility improvement.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Join NeuronMesh](docs/JOIN.md) to get started.
