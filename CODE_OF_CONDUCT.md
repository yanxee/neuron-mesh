# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

NeuronMesh is a research collaboration. We expect participants to make technical disagreement safe, specific and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not people.
- State assumptions, hardware, versions and failure cases when reporting results.
- Welcome questions from contributors with different backgrounds.
- Give actionable review comments and explain the tradeoff behind them.
- Respect privacy, model licenses, dataset licenses and participant consent.
- Credit prior work and external standards accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information, credentials or data.
- Deliberately submitting malicious models, poisoned updates, hidden telemetry or undisclosed network activity.
- Claiming unverified benchmarks, production readiness or exclusive ownership of community work.
- Dismissing safety, privacy or license concerns solely because they reduce performance.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat channels and project events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue.

Maintainers may request clarification, remove content, close discussions, reject contributions or temporarily restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when possible, and avoid retaliation.
